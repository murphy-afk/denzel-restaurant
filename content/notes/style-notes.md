# Denzel Style Notes

Derived from the menu photos, logo, and gallery images on denzel.it.

## Visual identity

- Dark, textured background: near-black slate or chalkboard texture (charcoal with subtle stone grain). The menu photos use a dark gray to black gradient with visible texture.
- Accent color: vivid grass green (approx. #3CB44A or similar). Used for section headers, prices, highlights, and the logo band.
- Secondary text: white and light gray on dark backgrounds.
- The logo: "DENZEL" in a classic serif font (similar to Playfair Display or a Didone style) in black on a green horizontal band, with "ART BURGER FISH & MORE" below in green serif caps.
- Decorative elements: hand-drawn chalk style illustrations (burger, salad bowl, steak, cake), diagonal hatch line borders, dotted separators, and sketch-style section titles that look like chalk lettering.

## Typography

- Section titles (ANTIPASTI, PANINI DENZEL, PIATTI DENZEL, SWEET DENZEL, BEVANDE E VINI): chalk hand-drawn display style, all caps, white.
- Dish names: bold sans-serif, all caps, green (#3CB44A).
- Descriptions: regular sans-serif, white, sentence case.
- Prices: bold sans-serif, green, aligned right.
- The overall feel is casual, urban, street food with a gourmet twist.

## Vibe and tone

- Confident, playful, informal. Example: "Sfida 530g" challenge, celebrity gallery, "Poldo ti fa un baffo?" copy.
- Kosher but fun: the site explains kosher rules with pride and humor (FAQ section).
- Gourmet street food: "street food di diverse parti del mondo in versione gourmet".
- Burgers served open so guests see exactly what they eat and dose the sauce themselves.
- The gallery shows a lively place with visitors, celebrities, events (birthdays, Hannukah, Miss Israel 2013).

## Color palette for the redesign

- Background: #1A1A1A (near black) or #212121 with subtle texture.
- Primary accent: #3CB44A (green).
- Text primary: #FFFFFF (white).
- Text secondary: #B0B0B0 (light gray).
- Optional warm accent for prices or highlights: keep green consistent.

## Photography style

- Food photos are warm, close-up, on wooden boards or dark surfaces.
- Gallery photos are candid, event-style, often with people.
- The menu photos themselves use the dark chalkboard aesthetic described above.

## Layout suggestions for the redesign

- Dark theme throughout to match the chalkboard vibe.
- Green accents for CTAs, prices, section headers.
- Chalk-style or handwritten display font for section titles (e.g., a Google Font like "Caveat" or "Permanent Marker" for accents, paired with a clean sans-serif like "Inter" or "Open Sans" for body).
- Serif logo treatment to keep the classic Denzel brand feel.
- Card-based layout for menu categories with the green headers.
